🎨 Colour legend

CAN CRUSH / DISPERSE — crush, disperse in water, or open capsule and give via enteral tube.
DO NOT CRUSH / DO NOT OPEN — modified-release, enteric-coated or soft-gel forms; risk of dose dumping or tube blockage.
ALTERNATIVE ROUTE / FORMULATION — a liquid, IV or alternative product is preferred.
CUT ONLY / SWALLOW WHOLE — tablet may be halved but not crushed.
CHECK PHARMACY — no clear instruction captured; verify before administering.
⚠️ Clinical safety note: Always confirm with the current product leaflet, your hospital formulary and a pharmacist before crushing or opening any medication. Cytotoxic, hormonal, and irritant drugs require special handling. Flush the enteral tube before and after each dose.
