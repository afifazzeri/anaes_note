  • Nephrogenic DI
  • Cerebral salt wasting
  • Post-obstructive diuresis
  • Recovery phase of ATN

Risk factors for DI Severe TBI (GCS<8) · head AIS>3 · base of skull fracture · cerebral oedema (esp. hypothalamus)

📋 Approach to polyuria & DDAVP use ›
  • Baseline serum sodium & osmolality
  • Watch urine output: 3 consecutive hours of ≥300 mL/hr
  • If sodium rising → send urine sodium & osmolality
  • Continue monitoring; sodium may safely approach 155 mmol/L
  • If sodium continues to rise + low urine Na + urine osmolality <300 mOsm/kg → consider DDAVP 0.5 µg
  • After DDAVP: monitor sodium frequently for 24–48 h
⚠️ DDAVP caution: risk of hyponatremia, especially during triphasic release of stored vasopressin. Historically linked to cerebral oedema (aquaporin‑4). Use lowest effective dose.

🔍 Trivial causes & specific diuresis types ›

Mannitol diuresis

Urine osmolality ↓ but not maximally dilute. Diuresis stops when mannitol ceases (unlike DI).

Hypertensive diuresis

Pressure natriuresis – increased renal perfusion pressure inhibits Na reabsorption. Seen when MAP transducer zeroed at Circle of Willis (sitting up → renal MAP 110–120).

Hypothermic diuresis

Non‑osmotic suppression of vasopressin; reversed by vasopressin analogues.

Normal response to fluid loading

Exclude if sodium keeps rising >150 mmol/L with dilute urine, or diuresis persists after water deprivation.

Hyperglycaemia

Stress response or dexamethasone; osmotic diuresis.

Cerebral salt wasting

Natriuresis, hypovolemia, low serum osmolarity; BNP not fully explanatory.

Nephrogenic DI

In trauma, most common cause is pre‑injury alcohol (zero‑order clearance ~1 drink/hr).

⚠️ Risks of hypernatremia & DDAVP – key numbers ›
  • Na⁺ >160 mmol/L independently associated with ↑ mortality (OR ≈ 29)
  • DI early in course → higher mortality; all patients who died developed DI within first 3 days
  • Complications of severe hypernatremia: thrombosis, renal failure, rebound cerebral oedema, seizures, delirium
  • DDAVP‑associated hyponatremia case series (children, enuresis) – cerebral oedema
  • Aquaporin‑4 knockout mice show less oedema – theoretical harm of DDAVP

Take home Target Na⁺ <155–160 mmol/L; use DDAVP judiciously, monitor Na⁺ closely.
